|---:|---|---|

| 2:30–2:40 | Repo, ZIP, Docker/Playground artifacts, closing rail | “Any WordPress site can expose safe agent workflows, measure where they succeed or fail, connect them to real outcomes, and improve the experience through WebMCP.” |

## Recording checklist

- Record the exact frozen tag and hosted database state.
- Keep browser zoom high enough to read tool names, evidence, order status, and policy result.
- Show actual calls and visible responses; do not substitute mockups.
- Ensure the timer is below 3:00 after the YouTube upload processes.
- Use original artwork and narration only; no copyrighted music.
- Open all public links logged out before and immediately after publishing.
