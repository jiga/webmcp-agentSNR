# Demonstration prompts

## Shopper workflow

```text
Find a waterproof backpack under $120, compare the two best choices,
confirm that the return policy is at least 30 days, and add the best-value
option to my cart.
```

## Unsupported request

```text
Notify me when the blue 24L version is back in stock.
```

## Merchant investigation

```text
Show the commerce funnel for my current workflow, identify the slowest
or failed tool call, explain what happened, and show any capabilities the
shopper requested that this store does not support.
```

## Session governance

```text
Disable product comparison for this demo session.
```
